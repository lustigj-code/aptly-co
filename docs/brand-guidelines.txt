to show the relationships among things or finite 
groups of things. Circles that overlap have a 
commonality while circles that do not overlap do 
not share those traits.



Venn diagrams help to visually represent the 
similarities and differences between two 
concepts.



Aptly’s logo represent the bases of their values 
and purpose: highest quality course content 
meets excellent student service.

DM Sans

Aptly uses DM Sans as the brand typeface.

It is is available in Google Fonts which

allows all users to download and respect the

brand’s visual identity.  

DM Sans is a low-contrast geometric sans serif design, 
intended for use at smaller text sizes.



DM Sans supports a Latin Extended glyph set, enabling 
typesetting for English and other Western European 
languages. It was designed by Colophon Foundry (UK), 
who started from the Latin portion of Indian Type 
Foundry's Poppins.



In May 2023 DM Sans is updated, expanding the 
coverage of the weight range to Thin & ExtraBlack (100–
1000 weight range) along with a new optical size axis.

Color Gradient

The gradient in our brand identity transitions 
smoothly from Navy to Light Teal, symbolizing our 
values of innovation and dynamism. It should be 
used primarily in backgrounds, overlays, and 
interactive elements, maintaining a consistent 
direction and ratio. 



Ensure high contrast when placing text over the 
gradient to preserve readability. Avoid altering the 
gradient colors, introducing new gradients, or 
using it in ways that diminish its impact or create 
visual clutter. Consistent use of this gradient 
enhances brand recognition and visual coherence 
across all media.

Circles

When using circles with fill and circles with just a 
stroke as part of a branding element, it's 
important to follow certain rules to maintain 
consistency and visual appeal. 



Use circles with fill and circles with stroke to 
create contrast and hierarchy within your design. 
For example, you can use filled circles for main 
elements or focal points, while using stroked 
circles for secondary or supporting elements.



Coordinate the colors of filled circles and strokes 
to create a cohesive color scheme. Consider using 
complementary or analogous colors to enhance 
visual impact while maintaining harmony.



All ornamental graphics must be always designed 
using the main color palette.

9.1

Stickers

Aptly Stickers serve as a component of the 
brand's print materials. When crafting any printed 
materials, it's essential to ensure that the logo is 
displayed and easily discernible. To prevent any 
unintended cropping or distortion of the logo, it's 
important to include sufficient white space 
around it.



Make sure you’re working within a CMYK color 
space to maintain consistency and accuracy in 
color reproduction throughout the printing 
process.
