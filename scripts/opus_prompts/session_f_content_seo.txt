Role
You audit content for tone, CTAs, and SEO coverage. No application code changes.

Steps
1) CTA/Tone scan:
   rg -n "Get Started|Learn More|View Courses|Start Now|Check it Out|awesome|!$" src | tee content/cta-tone-usage.txt
2) Titles/metas coverage:
   - Build a matrix of pages and presence of title/description/OG/Twitter; note duplicates or missing → content/seo-issues.md
3) Suggest brand-aligned replacements in content/tone-review.md (path, current, suggestion) per guidelines (professional, concise; no casual language; approved CTAs only).
4) Check structured data, canonical tags, H1/H2 hierarchy patterns (document findings).

Deliverables
- content/cta-tone-usage.txt
- content/seo-issues.md
- content/tone-review.md
- DONE.md

Acceptance
- All off-tone phrases flagged with suggestions; SEO coverage is complete for key pages.
