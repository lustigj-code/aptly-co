## 1. Logo System

### Logo Concept
- **Design**: Venn diagram with overlapping circles
- **Symbolism**: Intersection of highest quality course content and excellent student service
- **Philosophy**: Circles that overlap show commonality - represents Aptly's core values

### Logo Variants
- **Horizontal**: For left/right alignment
- **Vertical**: For central alignment 
- **Icon**: For small spaces and social media profiles
- **Wordmark**: Text-only version for client partnerships

### Logo Usage Rules
✅ **Allowed Backgrounds**: Navy, Teal, White
✅ **Intersection Color**: Always Yellow (#FFDE00)
✅ **Monochrome**: Black and white for B&W printing only

❌ **Prohibited Actions**:
- Using logo in stand-alone colors
- Rotating, scaling, or deforming
- Applying graphic effects
- Changing colors or opacity
- Using on complex backgrounds
- Modifying font, kerning, or spacing

### Logo Sizing Requirements
- **Print Minimum**: 7mm height (vertical), 5mm height (horizontal)
- **Web Minimum**: 24px height
- **Clearspace**: Equal to logo height on all sides
- **Client Logo Pairing**: Equal height, spacing = wordmark length ÷ 2

## 2. Typography System

### Primary Font: DM Sans (Google Fonts)
- **Weights Available**: Regular (400), Medium (500), Bold (700)
- **Character Set**: Full Latin Extended glyph set
- **Design**: Low-contrast geometric sans serif
- **Usage**: All brand communications
- **Restriction**: NEVER use any other font family

### Typography Hierarchy
- Crucial for brand consistency
- Must clearly scale and showcase information hierarchy
- Reference examples provided for proper implementation

## 3. Color Palette

### Primary Colors (Core Brand)
- **Navy**: #0A004A | RGB(10, 0, 74) | CMYK: C100% M95% Y37% K48%
- **Teal**: #21A8B0 | RGB(33, 168, 176) | CMYK: C74% M9% Y33% K0%
- **Yellow**: #FFDE00 | RGB(255, 222, 0) | CMYK: C2% M9% Y91% K0%

### Secondary Colors
- **Light Navy**: #3B336E | RGB(59, 51, 110)
- **Muted Teal**: #69BCC1 | RGB(105, 188, 193)
- **Light Teal**: #DEF2F2 | RGB(222, 242, 242)

### Monochrome Palette
- **Light Grey**: #E6E6E6 - UI elements, dividers
- **Grey**: #CCCCCC - Secondary text
- **Rich Black**: #333333 - Text on light backgrounds

### Brand Gradient
- **Flow**: Navy → Light Navy → Teal → Muted Teal → Light Teal
- **Usage**: Backgrounds, overlays, interactive elements
- **Tool**: https://cssgradient.io/
- **Requirement**: Maintain high contrast for text readability

## 4. Illustration Guidelines

### People Illustrations
- **Principle**: Diversity and inclusivity mandatory
- **Style**: Abstract to realistic depending on context
- **Expressions**: Faces and hands must be expressive
- **Shadows**: Required in clothing, optional for natural lighting
- **Representation**: Variety of roles, ages, backgrounds

### Animal Illustrations
- **Style**: Simple, flat, modern bodies
- **Eyes**: Always big and expressive
- **Context**: Always in scenes with relevant context
- **Behavior**: Can exhibit human behaviors (reading, studying)
- **Details**: Can use gradients for depth in tails/ears

### Color Requirements for Illustrations
- **Mandatory Colors**: Must include teal, purple (navy), and navy
- **Color Limit**: Maximum 6-7 colors per illustration
- **Secondary Elements**: Can be grey instead of full color
- **Palette**: 6 additional colors beyond brand palette available

### Skin Tone Diversity Palette
6 inclusive skin tones from light to dark:
- Light Pink (#FDE4D2) to Brown (#7D524E)
- Celebrates global diversity and representation

## 5. Photography Standards

### Image Selection Criteria
- **Source**: Adobe Stock (high-quality, curated)
- **Content**: People, lifestyle, digital devices, technology
- **Style**: Minimalistic over busy compositions
- **Focus**: Human-driven photography
- **Diversity**: Age, race, gender, sexuality representation
- **Treatment**: Rounded corners for video content

### Text Overlay Requirements
- **Rule**: Always apply dark gradient overlay for text on images
- **Purpose**: Ensure text readability and accessibility
- **Usage**: Sparingly, only when necessary
- **Graphics**: All ornamental graphics must use main color palette

## 6. Icon System

### Icon Design Rules
1. **Style**: Outline icons only
2. **Corners**: Rounded where possible
3. **Format**: Must fit in square format
4. **Details**: Avoid excessive detail
5. **Library**: Standardized icon library available

### Circular Graphics
- **Fill vs Stroke**: Filled circles for primary elements, stroked for secondary
- **Color Coordination**: Must use brand color palette
- **Hierarchy**: Create contrast and visual hierarchy
- **Templates**: Available in Google Slides and Figma

## 7. Social Media Guidelines

### Instagram Strategy
- **Approach**: Inspiration, education, community engagement
- **Visual Style**: Rich colors, inclusive illustrations
- **Content Types**: Milestones, course announcements, holidays, tips
- **Philosophy**: "Classroom without walls"

### Facebook Strategy
- **Alignment**: Same guidelines as Instagram
- **Content**: Milestones, course updates, insightful tips
- **Engagement**: Community interaction focus

### LinkedIn Strategy
- **Audience**: Professionals, businesses, founders, leaders
- **Content**: Blog posts, industry insights, thought leadership
- **Purpose**: Meaningful connections and knowledge sharing

## 8. UI Design Rules

### Mobile UI Requirements
- **Frame Rule**: ALL phone UIs must have phone frame
- **Purpose**: Production team needs frames for final video
- **Storyboard**: Place frames on every storyboard

### Desktop UI Requirements
- **Frame Rule**: ALL desktop UIs must have desktop frame
- **Purpose**: Production team reference for final output
- **Consistency**: Apply to every storyboard

### Highlight/Callout System
- **Color**: Aptly Yellow (#FFDE00) border
- **Style**: Rounded corners
- **Weight**: 8-12px border
- **Process**: Design team marks areas, Production team creates final effect

## 9. Print Guidelines

### General Print Requirements
- **Color Space**: CMYK only for all print materials
- **Logo**: Must be easily discernible with sufficient white space
- **Consistency**: Maintain brand identity across all materials

### Merchandise Standards
- **Vendor**: CustomInk for most items
- **Requirements**: Accurate logo size, placement, color representation
- **Process**: Review digital proofs before finalizing orders
- **Items**: Stickers, zip-up hoodies, water bottles

## 10. Voice & Tone Guidelines

### Communication Style
✅ **Required Characteristics**:
- Professional and authoritative
- Clear and concise
- Educational but accessible
- No casual language or slang
- No exclamation marks (except CTAs)

### Call-to-Action Standards
✅ **Approved**: "Get Started", "Learn More", "View Courses"
❌ **Avoid**: "Start Now!", "Check it Out", "Browse Our Awesome Courses"

## 11. Absolute Restrictions

### Never Use
- Colors outside the brand palette
- Fonts other than DM Sans
- Emojis in professional contexts
- Complex animations or effects
- Drop shadows (use subtle overlays only)
- Non-brand approved illustrations
- Gradients outside the specified brand gradient

### Always Required
- Brand color consistency
- DM Sans font family
- Professional tone
- WCAG AA accessibility compliance
- 8px spacing grid system
- Semantic HTML and ARIA labels
- Next.js Image component for web assets

## 12. Key Brand Values Reflected in Guidelines

1. **Diversity & Inclusion**: Mandatory in all visual representations
2. **Professionalism**: Maintained across all touchpoints
3. **Accessibility**: WCAG AA compliance required
4. **Educational Excellence**: Clear, readable, informative design
5. **Innovation**: Modern, clean, technology-forward aesthetic
6. **Community**: Human-centered, inclusive approach
