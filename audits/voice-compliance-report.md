# Voice Compliance Report - Aptly.co

## Executive Summary

**Overall Compliance Score: B+ (Good)**

The Aptly.co website demonstrates strong adherence to the brand voice guidelines with only minor violations found. The content maintains a professional, educational, and authoritative tone throughout the site. The main areas for improvement involve expanding contractions and ensuring consistent formality across all content.

## Brand Voice Requirements Analysis

### ✅ **Strengths**

1. **Professional & Authoritative Tone**
   - All major pages maintain professional language
   - No slang or overly casual language detected
   - Educational approach consistently used

2. **Clear & Concise Communication**
   - Content is well-structured and easy to understand
   - Technical concepts explained accessibly
   - Value propositions clearly stated

3. **Approved CTA Usage**
   - All CTAs use approved language patterns
   - "Get Started", "Learn More", "Browse Programs" used consistently
   - No prohibited phrases like "Check it Out" or "Start Now!" found

4. **Terminology Consistency**
   - Company name "Aptly" used consistently
   - Product names standardized across content
   - Certificate program names accurate and consistent

### ⚠️ **Areas for Improvement**

1. **Contractions Usage** (Minor Violations)
   - Homepage: "We don't just teach skills" → "We do not just teach skills"
   - Homepage: "you're job-ready" → "you are job-ready" 
   - About: "you're looking to transform" → "you are looking to transform"
   - Contact: "We're here to help" → "We are here to help"
   - FAQ: "You'll need a computer" → "You will need a computer"

2. **Form Placeholders** (Minor)
   - Contact form uses casual placeholders ("John Doe", "john@example.com")
   - Should use more professional examples

## Recommendations

### **Immediate Actions** (High Priority)
1. **Expand all contractions** to maintain formal tone:
   - don't → do not
   - you're → you are  
   - we're → we are
   - you'll → you will

2. **Update form placeholders** with professional examples:
   - "John Doe" → "Jane Smith"
   - "john@example.com" → "jane.smith@company.com"

### **Strategic Improvements** (Medium Priority)
1. **Develop content style guide** for future consistency
2. **Implement content review process** for new pages
3. **Create template language** for common sections
4. **Establish voice training** for content creators

### **Monitoring & Maintenance** (Ongoing)
1. **Regular content audits** for voice compliance
2. **Review new content** before publication
3. **Monitor user-generated content** (testimonials, reviews)
4. **Track industry terminology** updates
